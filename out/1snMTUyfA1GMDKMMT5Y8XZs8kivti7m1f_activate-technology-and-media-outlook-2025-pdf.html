TL;DR

Activate’s Technology & Media Outlook 2025 projects $1.1T in incremental global tech and media growth over four years, with consumer internet/media reaching ~$3.0T and B2B tech/software ~$3.2T by 2028. Generative AI is already addressing 60% of search activity, accelerating shifts in discovery. Advertising drives over half of sector growth, led by connected TV and retail media, with retailers surpassing $100B in ad revenue by 2028. Super Users (24% of adults) and rising daily digital time underpin spending across eCommerce, gaming, and video.

5 Insights

  1. Market expansion: Global consumer internet and media rises from $2.6T (2024) to $3.0T (2028), adding >$400B; B2B tech/software grows from $2.5T to $3.2T (+$729B). Note: another section cites +$800B for B2B spend—this discrepancy suggests different scopes (revenue vs. spend), and the exact uplift is uncertain.
  2. AI reshapes discovery: Generative AI already addresses 60% of search activity; 15M U.S. adults start searches on dedicated GenAI platforms today, forecast to 36M by 2028—implying traffic and budget shifts away from legacy search engines.
  3. Ad growth engines: Advertising accounts for >50% of global internet/media revenue growth (2024–2028), with connected TV and retail media as primary drivers; major retailers are expanding into CTV and will take in >$100B in ad revenue by 2028.
  4. eCommerce upside: Global eCommerce will add >$3T in growth dollars over four years; while the top 10 players drive >60% of global online GMV, >$2T remains up for grabs, as consumers adopt multi-channel journeys (including GenAI-powered platforms).
  5. Audience concentration: Super Users comprise 24% of adults and spend ~2x more time with tech/media than other users; they over-index on spend in eCommerce, gaming, music, and video and are earliest adopters—driving outsized conversion and LTV.

Commentary

For brands, the mix should tilt toward retail media and connected TV, using retailer first-party data to close the loop on attribution and improve ROAS. As GenAI captures a larger share of discovery, measurement must extend beyond legacy SEO/SEM to include AI assistants and multi-touch paths that start in chat or retail ecosystems. Targeting Super Users can yield disproportionate returns, but requires creative tailored to high-intent segments and rapid testing in ad-tier streaming and social video. Given large eCommerce whitespace (> $2T), diversification across marketplaces, retail media networks, and AI-guided shopping will be key, with incrementality measurement built into every placement.
