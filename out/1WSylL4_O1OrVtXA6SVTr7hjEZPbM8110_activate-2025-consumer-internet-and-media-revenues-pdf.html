TL;DR

Global consumer internet and media revenues are forecast to grow from $2.6T in 2024E to $3.0T in 2028E (+$403B; 3.7% CAGR). Advertising will drive more than half of that growth, outpacing paid content and internet access. Consumer spend rises from $1.7T to $1.9T, with subscriptions contributing the vast majority of the increase. Growth is broad-based but skewed toward ad-supported models and subscriptions.
