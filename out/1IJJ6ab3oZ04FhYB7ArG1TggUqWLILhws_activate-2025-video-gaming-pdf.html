5 Insights

  1. Global active gamers are forecast to reach over 3.5B by 2028, with gamer population growth accelerating from a 6% CAGR (2019–2024E) to 7% (2024E–2028E).
  2. Global consumer video game revenue is projected to surpass $200B by 2028 (excludes hardware/devices, AR/VR content, and advertising).
  3. Mobile accounts for more than half of game revenues today and is set to drive the most incremental growth; the 2028E mobile figure appears to be roughly $124B (approximation; exact figure inferred from chart and may be uncertain).
  4. Non-mobile platforms show stagnation: console and PC revenues appear flat to slightly down in 2021–2024E, with one platform showing about a -1% CAGR in that period (uncertain which), and only low single-digit growth returning by 2028E.
  5. Franchises dominate paid PC/console sales: the majority of top-earning titles (2021–2023) are sequels (e.g., Call of Duty, Madden, Zelda), with very few new franchises breaking into the top 10—implying established IP drives consumer spend.

Commentary

For brands and performance teams, the scale and growth of mobile gaming signal where reach and ROAS gains are most likely: invest in mobile in-app inventory, optimize IAP-driven campaigns, and deploy incrementality and LTV modeling to capture long-run value. Established console/PC franchises still deliver concentrated, high-intent audiences, making them attractive for premium partnerships and IP-aligned creative but less scalable for always-on performance. Measurement should align to the report’s revenue scope (content only—no hardware/AR/VR/ads), and account for privacy constraints in mobile (SKAN/MMM) with robust creative testing and audience suitability controls to sustain efficient acquisition.
