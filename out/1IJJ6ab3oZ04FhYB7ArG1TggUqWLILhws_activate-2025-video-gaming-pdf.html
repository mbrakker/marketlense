TL;DR

Gaming will surpass 3.5B active adults by 2028 as revenues top ~$200B. Growth slowed 2021–2024 but reaccelerates through 2028, led by mobile. Mobile already contributes over half of revenue and widens its lead. Top PC/console sellers remain dominated by sequels, with few new IP breakthroughs.

5 Insights

  1. Global gamer population is projected to exceed 3.5B by 2028E (≈3.59B vs. ≈3.36B in 2024E), implying ~6% CAGR (2019–2024E) and ~7% CAGR (2024E–2028E); figures may reflect rounding.
  2. Consumer video game revenue is forecast to be >$200B by 2028E (≈$217B total), after a flat 2021–2024E period (~1% CAGR) and then a reacceleration to ~6–7% CAGR into 2028E.
  3. Mobile is the majority revenue platform today and grows fastest: 2024E mobile ≈$87B of ≈$169B total (~51% share), reaching ≈$124B of ≈$217B in 2028E (~57% share); exact values may be approximate due to rounding.
  4. By region, 2028E revenue skews Rest of World (≈$157B) vs. U.S. (≈$61B), implying the U.S. at ~28% of global revenue; numbers may not sum exactly due to rounding per the source.
  5. Franchise dominance persists in paid PC/console top earners: across 2021–2023, most top-10 titles are sequels, with only a handful of new franchises breaking in (e.g., Elden Ring in 2022; possibly Hogwarts Legacy in 2023).

Commentary

For brands and performance marketers, the share and growth concentration in mobile suggests shifting budget toward mobile gaming inventory, with creative and measurement tailored for in-app environments (ID- and SKAN-aware attribution, incrementality, and MMM). Franchise dominance on PC/console implies dependable, high-reach launch windows around sequels for premium placements and co-promotions, while new IP bets remain riskier but can deliver breakout attention. Given muted 2021–2024 growth and reacceleration through 2028, plan for tighter short-term ROAS guardrails alongside long-term reach-building in mobile markets outside the U.S., where the majority of revenue resides. Align KPIs to platform realities (e.g., attention and view-through for mobile video; lift and sales outcomes around franchise release cycles on console/PC).
