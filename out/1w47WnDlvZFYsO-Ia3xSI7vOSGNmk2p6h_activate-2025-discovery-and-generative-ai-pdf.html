TL;DR

Generative AI is rapidly reshaping search, with up to 60% of queries already addressable today. In the U.S., 15M adults currently start searches on dedicated GenAI platforms, forecast to reach 36M by 2028 (26% CAGR). Users lean on GenAI for exploration and factual queries more than navigation. Full disruption of gated content and transactions depends on licensing and payment/authentication capabilities.

5 Insights

  1. Up to 60% of searches are already addressable through generative AI; the remaining 40% are expected to become addressable as data access and capabilities expand (Activate analysis, SparkToro).
  2. In the U.S., 15M adults currently start online searches on dedicated generative AI platforms; Activate forecasts 36M by 2028 (2024–2028E CAGR: 26%).
  3. Among monthly users of both GenAI search and traditional engines, the report indicates about 68% use GenAI for exploration, 62% for direct inquiries, and 32% for navigation—this mapping is inferred from chart order and may be approximate given the text-only extract.
  4. Near-term likelihood of GenAI becoming the default tool is HIGH for learning, recommendations, writing improvement, summarization, and translation; MID for organizing daily life and personalized plans; LOW for entertainment and design/editing where specialized tools dominate.
  5. GenAI’s ability to disrupt gated content and transaction-led queries depends on partnerships/licensing with publishers and on enabling payments and product authenticity verification; without these, disruption concentrates in open-web discovery.

Commentary

Brands should prepare for fewer click-throughs from traditional SERPs as exploratory and factual intent shifts to GenAI answers. Optimize content for LLM ingestion (clear structure, citations, up-to-date data) and pursue data partnerships so brand information appears accurately within GenAI responses. Measurement must evolve to include exposure and assistance within conversational interfaces, using holdouts and MMM to estimate incremental lift when clicks are suppressed. For ROAS, expect higher value from upper-funnel GenAI placements and from authenticated product feeds that enable downstream conversions as payment and verification layers mature.
