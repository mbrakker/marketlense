TL;DR

Generative AI is rapidly reshaping online discovery, with up to 60% of searches already addressable via AI and the balance expected as access to gated content and transactions evolves. In the U.S., users who start searches on dedicated AI platforms are projected to grow from 15M today to 36M by 2028 (26% CAGR). Consumers already prefer AI for exploration and direct fact retrieval over navigation.

5 Insights

  1. Up to 60% of searches are already addressable through generative AI, signaling significant substitution for traditional search in many query types.
  2. The remaining 40% of searches will be addressable as the market moves into gated content and transaction flows, which require publisher licensing partnerships and capabilities like payment processing and product authenticity verification.
  3. 15M U.S. adults currently begin searches on dedicated generative AI platforms, with a forecast of 36M by 2028 (2024–2028E CAGR: 26%).
  4. Among monthly users of both AI and traditional search, 68% use generative AI for exploration (e.g., learning about a topic).
  5. Within the same cohort, 62% use generative AI for direct inquiry versus 32% for navigation, indicating AI is displacing knowledge retrieval more than site-finding.

Commentary

Brands should begin optimizing content for LLM-driven discovery (structured, verifiable, up-to-date) and negotiate licensing to ensure inclusion in gated environments where AI summaries will source data. Media plans need budgets for AI-native surfaces and answer engines, with measurement frameworks that capture exploration and direct-inquiry use cases (beyond last-click), including assistant-originated traffic and AI-influenced conversions. Expect ROAS to hinge on prompt-aligned creative, schema-rich content, and presence in AI shopping/booking flows as transactions move into assistants. Given lower AI usage for pure navigation, defensive spend on branded SEM may be less efficient, while upper/mid-funnel AI answer placements and affiliate-like AI recommendations could drive incremental lift.
