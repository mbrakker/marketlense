### Demonstrated current evidence-fidelity loss

`evidence_fidelity.json` contains 28 candidates. All 16 unsupported candidates in the findings pack have a complete page reference and fail only `quote_match: quote_not_matched`; all 16 are classified as `quotation`. The candidate text wraps an explanatory proposition in a quoted chart/table title. The current `_claim_kind()` returns `quotation` as soon as it finds any quoted span, before quantity detection (`src/generators/claim_validation_generator.py:187–193`). The quotation branch checks quoted text only (`:635–657`); because the claim is no longer classified as numeric, `number_value_unit_match` does not run. `src/generators/evidence_pack_generator.py:1201–1272` validates these packs and then removes every non-supported candidate. This is the first demonstrated loss stage; the final findings, editorial plan, insight selector and copy see no such candidate. Their omission therefore does not establish a selector defect.

For the offline counterfactual, the 16 exact candidate texts and page IDs were loaded from the retained fidelity ledger, and the current public `validate_evidence_fidelity()` was called with page text extracted from the frozen PDF using PyMuPDF (no semantic callback or provider). With original text, the replay reproduced 16/16 `quote_not_matched`. Removing only quote delimiters around chart/table headings, without changing the proposition, yielded 12 supported, 3 unsupported and 1 unresolved. The three p. 13 claims say Google Search referrals were “down” 33% globally, 38% in the US and 17% in Europe year-on-year from November 2024 to November 2025. The physical page’s table maps those exact signed values to the three exact regions and timeframe. The unchanged phrase “down 33%” is parsed as positive 33 and fails against source `-33%`; expressing that same proposition as signed `-33%` in a second replay makes all three pass. This is a demonstrated direction-word false rejection after the quote wrapper is removed, not a reason to accept an unsupported number.
