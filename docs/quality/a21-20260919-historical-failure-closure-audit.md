The fixture first failed on that conflict and now proves the target remains the EMEA insight, the sibling and its evidence remain unchanged, and the repair plan keeps the valid source available. It does **not** claim that the old model candidate repairs: candidate 1 still fails the unchanged duplicate gate, and no retained, fully validated fifth replacement insight exists. A new bounded model repair remains necessary to prove end-to-end closure.
