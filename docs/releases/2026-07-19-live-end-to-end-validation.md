## Publisher discovery

The isolated database contains 120 publisher records after synchronising the 83 configured publisher profiles and retaining live-discovery metadata. Discovery targeted 20 publishers across consulting, retail/commerce, media measurement, advertising, social research, financial services, and technology.

- 14 discovery runs were accepted. Successful mechanisms included `http_parse` and `browser_render`; duplicate/current-report screening was applied before persistence.
- The accepted sources yielded 1,606 current reports and 429 qualified new reports across the retained discovery rows.
- Two sources were deliberately retained as `raw_only_delta_rejected` rather than promoted from weak deltas (GWI and Nielsen).
- Six target discoveries did not complete: five failed with `llm_execution_policy_unknown_namespace` (Bain, Brand Finance, LiveRamp, Mintel, and Commerce Tools); Beano Brain stopped with `publisher_inventory_browser_incomplete` after its undercoverage guard fired.

An early queue-interface probe was also recorded in the isolated state only: the queue handler required a pre-existing publisher ID while the public discovery command accepts a URL. One job was correctly dead-lettered as `publisher_inventory_publisher_not_found` and the remaining 19 queued probes were cancelled. This was not retried as a discovery route.

## Known issues and recommendations

1. Restore runtime parity between `browser-doctor` and the acquisition worker. The doctor passed, but every browser-dependent acquisition route failed importing/initialising `browser_use`.
2. Complete explicit model-policy coverage for the five discovery namespaces rejected by `llm_execution_policy_unknown_namespace`; this is direct live evidence for existing backlog item A15.
3. Diagnose why freshly projected, passing report packages do not satisfy the final canonical-LLM-projection freshness/boundedness requirement. Keep the WordPress fail-closed rule intact; fix the evidence handoff, then re-run only the publication preflight against the retained packages.
4. Harden the context-category JSON contract and cover-fingerprint geometry handling before expanding the trusted corpus. These failures were correctly visible and stopped final completion.
5. Preserve the strict PDF budget. For the follow-up, begin a new governed run or requeue eligible deferred work rather than raising the limit in-place.
6. Reconcile per-stage LLM ledger attribution so future validation reports can distinguish discovery, processing, and editorial cost without log reconstruction.
