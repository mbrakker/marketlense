01. Overview

Activate Technology Media Outlook 2026

Executive summary

Executives should treat AI as an expansion of existing discovery and operating models, not an immediate replacement for traditional search or established capabilities. Generative AI reached 128 million U.S. adult monthly users in 2025, up 45 million year over year, and the report projects adoption across the entire internet population within five years. Online shoppers report interest in using generative AI for discovery, research, and purchasing, at 69%, 71%, and 63%, respectively, while 60% are interested in using it throughout the buying journey. Super Users, who represent 28% of the U.S. population, spend 17:06 a day with technology and media versus 9:31 for other users and are substantially more likely to adopt spatial-computing devices. Enterprise buyers expect technology spending to increase over the next five years, with expectations from 84% of enterprise buyers, 89% of mid-market buyers, and 77% of SMB buyers, while AI and data-capability adoption are identified as leading drivers. Capturing that value will require clear measurable ROI targets, data and security capabilities, and new operating frameworks, with the enterprise findings based on a U.S. survey of 732 technology decision-makers.

05. Expert view

MarketBearing expert view.

Our team's perspective translates the report into practical implications for business, brand and digital leaders.

MB

MarketBearing analysis

Generative AI is expanding the search journey rather than removing the role of traditional search. In 2025, usage reached 128 million monthly U.S. adult users, up 45 million year over year, while the reported journey places AI in ideation and synthesis but traditional search in research and validation. For ecommerce and digital leaders, the decision is to coordinate discovery, validation, and planning across both environments rather than treat AI search as a standalone replacement. The most important early audience signal may be concentrated among Super Users. They represent 28% of the population but record 17:06 of daily technology and media time, compared with 9:31 for other users, and their adoption is indexed at 4.3x for monthly VR/MR headset and AI-glasses use. Leaders assessing emerging interfaces should therefore examine Super User behavior separately from aggregate adoption, particularly as spatial computing is positioned as a potential post-smartphone platform. Investment appetite is broad, but the value test is becoming more demanding. Expected technology-spend increases over the next five years are 84% among enterprise buyers, 89% among mid-market buyers, and 77% among SMB buyers, with AI capability adoption identified as the leading driver, while decision makers expect tangible and measurable value from those investments. Spending plans therefore need to be paired with clear ROI targets, data and security capabilities, and operating frameworks, which the evidence identifies as requirements for maximizing AI investment value.
in LinkedIn-ready post
AI investment is becoming a broad buyer expectation, but expected spend is not the same as realized value. That tension is one representative angle from the Activate Technology & Media Outlook 2026, not a summary of the full report. The report finds that 89% of mid-market buyers expect technology spending to increase over the next five years. The expectation is also present among 84% of enterprise buyers and 77% of SMB buyers, with AI capability adoption identified as the leading driver of that expected growth. The implication for operators is that AI investment is being considered across company sizes, while the basis for investment remains value. The report’s enterprise research says companies will need clear, measurable ROI targets, data and security capabilities, and new operating frameworks to maximize the value of AI investments. That creates a higher bar than selecting a model or launching an experiment. The operating conditions around AI matter because value realization depends on more than the technology itself: objectives must be measurable, and the business must have the data, security capabilities, and operating frameworks required to support the investment. The opportunity is therefore paired with a discipline requirement. Buyers may expect technology budgets to rise, but durable AI value depends on whether organizations can define measurable outcomes and establish the capabilities needed to pursue them. #EnterpriseAI #TechnologyStrategy
