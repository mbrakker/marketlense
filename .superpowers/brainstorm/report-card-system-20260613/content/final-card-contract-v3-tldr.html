TLDR correction

Every card shows a complete TLDR. Nothing is clipped, clamped, or ellipsized.

The cards stay aligned by reserving explicit content rows and validating TLDR length before publication. The remaining choice is whether small cards carry the same standard TLDR or a complete compact TLDR written for their density.

!

Why a content contract is required: current report data includes a roughly 180-word source thesis. Rendering arbitrary source length in fixed aligned cards is impossible without either uncontrolled card growth or truncation. The pipeline must persist bounded, semantically complete card TLDRs.
