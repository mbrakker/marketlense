Privacy notice

Market Bearing stores operational metadata required to ingest reports, validate artifacts, and publish approved intelligence to WordPress.

Data we process

  • Report source metadata (file identifiers, hashes, publish states).
  • Generated content artifacts (summaries, metrics, quote candidates, rendered HTML).
  • Operational telemetry (structured run/task/span logs and validation outputs).

Security controls

Secrets are loaded from environment variables, not from committed theme code. Logs are structured and must redact sensitive tokens and private credentials before storage.
