Submit a report for structured review and report brief creation

Share the source file, context, and timing expectations up front so the Market Bearing pipeline can validate, prioritize, and publish the report with the right metadata and evidence checks.

Required

Source package

Provide the report file URL or upload location, plus publisher, publication date, and region when those details are known.

Context

Why it matters now

Explain the business question, audience, or active decision that makes this report worth processing now rather than later.

Timing

Priority guidance

Flag urgent sections, deadlines, or expected deliverables if the report needs accelerated review or a narrower editorial cut.

What happens next

  • The report is checked for extractable text, content quality, and evidence integrity before report brief creation begins.
  • Submissions missing critical metadata or clear source material may be paused until the source package is improved.
  • Accepted submissions are processed through the same validation and publication gates as scheduled ingestion.
