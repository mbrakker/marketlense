Terms of use

Published digests are for internal decision support. Teams should validate high-stakes decisions against original source material.

Use constraints

  • Do not represent generated summaries as verbatim source text.
  • Do not remove citations when redistributing insights outside your organization.
  • Do not publish sensitive inputs that are restricted by source licenses.

Service reliability

Pipeline retries, validation policies, and publishing rules are managed by orchestrators and may block outputs that fail quality gates. Blocked outputs are not considered final deliverables.
